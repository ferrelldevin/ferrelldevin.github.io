Study Kit

Open site Source

Free study sheets for ODU Japanese courses. Built from the assigned lessons only, no romaji.

  • Kurikaeshiapp Spaced-repetition study cards for any course. Runs on your phone, works offline, keeps nothing anywhere but your own browser. Version 0.5.1. /studykit/kurikaeshi/
  • Kurikaeshi for your classlive For the instructor. Written to be forwarded to students as-is; the first half is theirs. /studykit/kurikaeshi/for-your-class.html
  • Say It Firstlive JAPN 301 Lesson 1 self-introduction written out, with menus of ready-made options, so speaking is reading aloud rather than composing. /studykit/japn301/say-it-first/
  • Kanji Quiz Prep, L1 to L4live All 64 kanji of Kanji Quiz 1: a cold sheet first, then tap-to-reveal drills for only what you missed. /studykit/japn309/kanji-quiz-prep/

Veteran Resources

Open site Source

Organized, cited pages and links for veteran students and the people who help them. The office hub.

  • Veteran Benefits Kit · ODUlive The front door to the kit: the three pages a veteran student, a work-study desk, or a counselor needs, and the VA and ODU pages behind them. One link to bookmark. /veteran-resources/education/odu-benefits-kit/
  • Which Benefit Am I Onlive VA education benefits side by side: who each chapter is for, what it pays and to whom, the current rate with its dates, the role to ask. /veteran-resources/education/odu-benefits-kit/benefit/
  • Benefits Front-Desk Maplive Fourteen situations a veteran student brings to the desk, each with one check, the branches, the action or hand-over, and the source beside it. /veteran-resources/education/odu-benefits-kit/desk/
  • Semester Pay Plannerapp Enter your classes; see, for each stretch of the term, whether you are above half time, whether you keep the in-person housing rate, and the monthly figure that follows. /veteran-resources/education/odu-benefits-kit/planner/
  • QR codeslive One code per page: SVG for a poster or door sign, PNG for a document or slide, and a ready-made card PDF with the address typed out. /veteran-resources/education/odu-benefits-kit/qr/
  • Office scan cardprint For the desk, not the student handout. Tape it inside the drawer or by the monitor; the code opens the live map with every line's sources. /veteran-resources/education/odu-benefits-kit/qr/office-scan-card.html
  • Student scan sheetprint Scan one. Starts with the front door if you are not sure; every page shows the answer first and the VA rule one tap away. /veteran-resources/education/odu-benefits-kit/qr/student-scan-sheet.html
  • Education benefitslive Which VA education benefit you are on, what a semester pays, and what the certifying office does with each question. Front door to the kit's four pages. /veteran-resources/education/
  • Claims, ratings and helplive Where a disability claim, a rating question, or any question in writing goes, and who may charge you nothing. Issuer links only; nothing built here yet. /veteran-resources/claims/
  • Health carelive Enrolling in VA health care, what it covers, and where to go, including mental health, which does not wait for enrollment. Issuer links only. /veteran-resources/health/
  • Home loanslive The VA-backed home loan: what it is, whether you qualify, and the Certificate of Eligibility the lender asks for. Issuer links only. /veteran-resources/home-loans/
  • Careers and employmentlive Getting from service to work: VA's employment programs, and tools for the paperwork, including the resume generator whose build fails on a claim it cannot defend. /veteran-resources/careers/
  • Transition assistancelive Leaving service, or recently out: the programs that run before and after the last day, and the counseling that is free either way. Issuer links only. /veteran-resources/transition/
  • Study sheets for ODU courseslive Not veteran-specific, but built for the same students: the Study Kit sheets, reached from the veteran side. /veteran-resources/study/
  • Printoutsprint Every page as a PDF, ready to print for a veteran or save to your system. Each printed page carries its own sources. /veteran-resources/print/
