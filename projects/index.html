Kurikaeshi

A spaced-repetition Japanese study app. It installs to a phone, works offline, and can sync study history to an optional account.

Open the app
  • JavaScript
  • FSRS scheduling
  • Service worker
  • Auth0
  • Neon Postgres
Try it
Tap the sample card, then start studying. No account needed. Installed to a home screen, it keeps working in airplane mode.
Built with
FSRS decides when each card comes back. The service worker keeps it running offline. Sign-in is optional, and synced study history sits in Neon Postgres with row-level security.
How it ships
Automated tests run the app in a headless browser. Then an AI agent uses it the way its users would, never sees the source, and cites a screenshot for every finding. Last, a judge from a different AI vendor returns a pass-or-fail verdict. More than 80 judged rounds so far; most failed and led to fixes.

Veteran Benefits Kit

Four pages for veteran students at ODU and the staff who help them: which VA education benefit you're on, a front-desk map of fourteen common situations, a semester pay planner, and QR codes for posters.

Try it
The planner shows, for each stretch of the term, whether you're above half time and the monthly figure that follows.
Built with
Every rate and rule links to the VA page or federal regulation it comes from, so any figure can be checked at the source.
How it ships
The three main pages each have a gate of hundreds of automated checks, a cross-page check holds the kit to the same figures, and each release of the main pages goes to an independent AI judge. The 2027 fiscal-year rates were staged in September behind a date check that refused to publish them before October 1.
Release story · a fraction rounded down
$0→$1,458 monthly figure for a 3-credit, eight-week class · the regulation keeps 6.75, the planner had floored it

The 2027 update found it, and it's fixed. A second lesson: In VA's drop-date rules, review kept finding one more branch. So the pages now cite the rules and send the student to the certifying official instead of guessing a date.

When a summary keeps failing review, cite the rule instead of paraphrasing it.

The search for banned words anchored on word boundaries, so a banned form could hide inside a longer word, and one did. It was found by scanning the files before publishing, not by the check. The check now matches endings.
